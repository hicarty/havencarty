The edge

Trained to design objects. Working as an engineer.

Most teams split the hardware and the app between people who rarely meet. I bring three disciplines to the same table, so the decisions that cross the physical and digital line get made once, early.

  • Industrial design

    My academic grounding. Form, materials, ergonomics, and how a product feels in the hand before anyone opens an app.

  • Software engineering

    I build what I design. I understand what the device, the data pipeline and the app can actually do, and what they cost to change.

  • UX and Agile project management

    Research-led design, delivered in sprints, so the physical and digital teams ship against one backlog and one definition of done.
